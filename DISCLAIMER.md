# Legal Disclaimer

## Intended Use

Capture Bypass is a developer and power-user utility for removing the
`WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE` display-affinity flag from windows running on your
own machine. Legitimate use cases include:

- Recording or streaming software you own or are licensed to use.
- Accessibility tooling that requires screen content of any window.
- Security research and testing of capture-protection implementations.
- Creating tutorial or documentation recordings of your own workflows.
